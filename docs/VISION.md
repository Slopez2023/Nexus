- Read and comprehend every line of code

### 4. Maintain Human Control

## 📊 Strategy Development Vision

### Deliberate Strategy Creation
1. **Research Thoroughly**: Understand the strategy's theoretical edge
2. **Document Clearly**: Write down exact entry/exit rules
3. **Implement Cleanly**: Code with clarity and error handling
4. **Validate Rigorously**: Backtest across multiple conditions
5. **Paper Trade**: Validate in real-time simulation
6. **Deploy Gradually**: Start tiny, scale based on results

### Strategy Requirements
**No strategy goes live without meeting ALL criteria:**
- ✅ **1000+ trades** in backtest sample
- ✅ **Sharpe ratio > 1.5** (excellent risk-adjusted returns)
- ✅ **Max drawdown < 20%** (controlled risk)
- ✅ **Profit factor > 1.3** (profitable edge)
- ✅ **Paper trading validation** matches backtest ±10%

### Strategy Categories
We build strategies we understand deeply:
- **Mean Reversion**: RSI, Bollinger Bands, statistical arbitrage
- **Trend Following**: Moving averages, momentum indicators
- **Breakout**: Volatility-based entry systems
- **Machine Learning**: Models we can explain and validate

## 🛡️ Risk Management Vision

### Defense in Depth
Multiple layers of protection:
- **Position Limits**: Max size per trade and symbol
- **Portfolio Limits**: Max drawdown and correlation exposure
- **Time Limits**: Trading halts during extreme volatility
- **Capital Limits**: Strict boundaries on total exposure

### Emergency Controls
- **Instant shutdown**: Stop all trading immediately
- **Position unwinding**: Close all positions on command
- **Cash reserves**: Minimum balance requirements
- **Circuit breakers**: Automatic halts on loss thresholds

### Risk Transparency
